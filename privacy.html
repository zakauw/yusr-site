Yusr Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Yusr is a free Android app that shows short Quran verses, one swipe at a time. It is made by [Developer name] ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

In short

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Your settings, saved verses and streak stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads.
  • We do not sell your information.

Information kept on your phone

To work, the app stores the following on your phone only:

  • Your choices: topics, daily goal, reminder time, text size and theme.
  • Your progress: which verses you have seen, how many today, and your streak.
  • The verses you save.
  • Whether you have finished the welcome screens or already seen a tip.
  • The date you first opened the app and how many verses you have shared, used to decide when to offer a reminder again or ask for a rating.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. To delete it, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. The app has no server of its own and no analytics.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. Ads appear as a card in the feed, labelled "Sponsored". There are no ads on shared images, in "Read in context", in the share screen or in the welcome screens.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and similar identifiers;
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address;
  • information about your device and the app, such as model, operating system and crash diagnostics;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps.

This information is sent to Google, encrypted in transit, and handled under Google's own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

We ask Google to show only ads suitable for a general audience and block sensitive ad categories such as gambling, dating and alcohol.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, "Ad privacy options". Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings, Privacy, Ads, or Settings, Google, Ads), which stops personalised ads based on it.

Daily reminder

If you turn on reminders, the reminder is scheduled on your phone by Android. No information is sent anywhere to do this. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in Android's notification settings.

Sharing verses

When you share a verse, Android's share menu sends the image or text to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp. We do not see what you share or who you share it with. Shared text includes a link to the app on Google Play with a tag that tells Google Play the install came from a share. The tag contains no information about you.

Ratings

The app may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review feature. Google handles your rating and review under its own policies; we only see what Google Play shows to all developers.

Children

The verses in the app are suitable for all ages, but the app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023, the EU and UK GDPR, and similar laws give you rights over your personal information, including access and deletion. We hold no personal information about you. For information Google holds in connection with ads, you can use Google's tools at myaccount.google.com or contact Google. If you have a question or request, contact us and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts using new services, for example crash reporting or an online translation service, we will update this policy and the date at the top before that version is released.
